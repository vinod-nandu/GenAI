7-Day South Indian Meal Plan
Role

Act as a qualified dietitian and nutrition planner specializing in balanced South Indian cuisine and meal planning for working professionals.

Task

Prepare a 7-day meal plan for working professionals in a clear tabular format.

The meal plan should:

Cover 7 complete days.
Include breakfast, mid-morning snack, lunch, evening snack, and dinner.
Avoid repeating the same meal or main dish during the 7 days.
Provide sufficient variety so the meals remain interesting and enjoyable.
Focus on meals that are practical and reasonably easy to prepare for working professionals.
Context

The target audience is South Indian working professionals.

Design the meal plan using primarily South Indian cuisine, including appropriate vegetarian and/or non-vegetarian options.

The meals should aim to provide:

Adequate calories for daily energy requirements.
Balanced carbohydrates, proteins, healthy fats, fiber, vitamins, and minerals.
A good balance of protein and complex carbohydrates.
Appropriate portions for working professionals.
Nutritious and minimally processed food choices.
Variety in ingredients and preparation methods.

Avoid excessive:

Sugar
Deep-fried foods
Highly processed foods
Excess salt
Refined carbohydrates

Daily Total Fiber
Important Assumptions

If the user's age, gender, height, weight, activity level, dietary restrictions, or calorie target are not provided:

Clearly state the assumptions used.
Use a reasonable general calorie target for a typical working adult.
Clearly label nutritional values as approximate estimates.
Do not present the plan as individualized medical or therapeutic nutrition advice.
Quality Check

Before presenting the final meal plan, verify that:

All 7 days are included.
Meals have good variety.
The same main dish is not repeated.
Each day contains all 5 meal categories.
The estimated calorie and nutrition values are internally consistent.
Meals are predominantly South Indian.
The plan is practical for working professionals.
The meals provide a reasonable balance of macronutrients and fiber.
