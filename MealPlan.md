# 7-Day South Indian Meal Plan

### Assumptions

* Designed for a **typical healthy working adult** with moderate daily activity.
* General target: approximately **1,900–2,100 kcal/day**.
* Portions are indicative and should be adjusted based on age, gender, height, weight, activity level and individual goals.
* Nutrition values are **approximate estimates**, not individualized medical or therapeutic advice.
* Water, buttermilk, unsweetened tea/coffee and other low-calorie beverages are assumed separately unless mentioned.
* The plan emphasizes **South Indian foods, whole grains, millets, pulses, vegetables, fruits, eggs, fish and lean chicken**.
* Cooking oil is assumed to be used moderately, roughly **3–4 tsp/day per person**.

### Overall balance

* **Protein:** Good general level for a moderately active working adult, distributed across most meals.
* **Carbohydrates:** Primarily from rice, millets, pulses, dosa/idli, fruits and vegetables rather than heavily processed foods.
* **Healthy fats:** Mainly from nuts, seeds, peanuts, fish and moderate cooking oil.
* **Fiber:** Strong contribution from vegetables, legumes, fruits, whole grains and millets.
* **Variety:** Uses different grains, pulses, vegetables and protein sources throughout the week.

# Practical Tips

1. **Batch preparation:** Cook dal, legumes, chopped vegetables and basic gravies in advance for 2–3 days to reduce weekday cooking time.
2. **Control rice portions:** A practical starting point is around **1–1.5 cups cooked rice** at lunch, adjusting according to activity and calorie needs.
3. **Prioritize protein:** Include eggs, dal, legumes, curd, paneer/tofu, fish or chicken in appropriate portions rather than making rice the dominant part of every meal.
4. **Healthy snacks:** Keep roasted chana, sundal, fruit, nuts, seeds or buttermilk available instead of biscuits, sweets and fried snacks.
5. **Hydration:** Aim for roughly **2–2.5 L of fluids/day**, with additional fluids when exercising or working in hot conditions.
6. **Reduce hidden calories:** Keep coconut chutney, fried accompaniments, excess oil, ghee and sugary beverages moderate.
7. **Busy-workday strategy:** If lunch preparation is difficult, prepare the main grain/protein the previous evening and assemble vegetables/curd/salad quickly in the morning.
